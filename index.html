Our company

Made in Illinois.
Built for markets across America.

Since 2020, Meat & Dough Foods LLC has manufactured frozen Eastern European-style comfort foods in Schaumburg, Illinois. Our portfolio brings together distinct brands, retail and foodservice formats, HMS Zabiha Halal-certified products and private-label manufacturing capability.

We serve distributors, retailers, foodservice operators and private-label partners across Chicagoland and throughout the United States. Every program is supported by practical product knowledge, clear communication and dependable domestic manufacturing.
